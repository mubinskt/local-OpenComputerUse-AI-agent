1. Click the Windows search field next to the Start button.
2. Type "Notepad" into the search box.
3. Press Enter to launch Notepad.
4. Wait for Notepad to open.
5. Type "Hello World" into the Notepad window.
6. Click the "File" menu in Notepad.
7. Select "Save As" and enter a filename such as "test_document.txt".
8. Press Enter to save the file.
